Inside the Book

Three sunny safety stories

Playful habits for sunscreen, hats, and drinking water.

01

The Sunshine Superpower

Yan learns how sunscreen protects his skin and helps him enjoy sunny days safely.

02

Hedgie’s Cool Hat

03

Red Birdie’s Water Bottle

Red Birdie learns why drinking water from her own clean bottle keeps her body healthy and ready to play.

Chasing your child around the house is fun.

Chasing them every morning to put on sunscreen… not so much.

Or reminding them to wear a hat.

Or asking, “Did you drink your water?”

again…

and again…

What if your child wanted to do those things on their own?

Yan the Little Squirrel: Safe Summer Adventures helps children understand why healthy habits matter through three playful stories they’ll want to read again and again. Parents will also discover simple, evidence-informed strategies to turn sunscreen, hats, and drinking water into everyday habits children can do more independently.

Inside you’ll discover:

The Sunshine Superpower
Yan learns how sunscreen protects his skin and helps him enjoy sunny days safely.

Hedgie’s Cool Hat
Hedgie discovers why wearing a hat keeps him cool and comfortable during hot summer adventures.

Red Birdie’s Water Bottle
Red Birdie learns why drinking water from her own clean water bottle keeps her body healthy and ready to play.

This interactive book also includes:

  • Cut-out stickers for hands-on learning
  • Fun facts about summer safety
  • Practical parent tips based on child development and habit-building research

Perfect for toddlers and preschoolers ages 2–5, this beautifully illustrated book helps children build healthy habits that can last a lifetime.

Because the best summer adventures begin with healthy habits.
