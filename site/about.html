About Anna

Anna Klimentova is a children’s author and social work professional, and the creator of the Wise Turtle Universe — a collection of emotionally intelligent stories that support children, teens, and adults through everyday challenges.

Her work focuses on emotional regulation, gentle parenting, toddler behaviour, and parent-child connection, helping families navigate tantrums, transitions, and big feelings through warm, relatable storytelling.

Her Books

Yan the Little Squirrel — gentle stories for toddlers and parents about emotions, routines, and connection.

Fira & Agate: Forest Adventures — stories for teens exploring identity, belonging, and resilience.

Snowly Tales: Everyday Calm — reflective stories for adults to navigate daily life with more ease, clarity, and balance.
