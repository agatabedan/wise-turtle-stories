Inside the Book

A gentle story about feeling better

Reassurance for little readers when illness feels new or worrying.

When Yan, the little squirrel, feels sick for the first time, he wonders what is happening inside his body.

With Mommy’s gentle reassurance, he learns about the tiny helpers that fight germs, the importance of rest and water, and why feeling sick doesn’t last forever.

A comforting story that helps young children understand illness and recovery.
