Inside the Book

Three real-life toddler challenges

Simple, repeatable calming steps for parents and toddlers to practice together.

01

Yan Who Said No

For toddlers with a strong urge to say "no," helping parents reduce resistance and navigate defiance using gentle parenting strategies.

02

Yan Who Could Not Wait

A tantrum story about waiting, teaching emotional regulation skills and practical tools to make waiting time easier for young children.

03

Yan Who Missed You

A reassuring story about separation anxiety, offering connection-based approaches to make transitions smoother for both child and caregiver.

"No!" battles that feel endless. Waiting that turns into meltdowns. Goodbyes filled with tears.

In Yan The Little Squirrel: Big Feelings, Little Choices, toddlers learn simple, repeatable calming steps through three relatable stories designed for real-life parenting challenges.

This collection supports:

  • Toddler emotional regulation
  • Co-regulation between parent and child
  • Managing toddler tantrums
  • Reducing power struggles
  • Easing separation anxiety
  • Positive discipline for ages 2-5

Written in a calming, bedtime-friendly rhythm, this picture book models repair after hard moments without shame, punishment, or overwhelm.

A practical toddler behavior book for parents seeking gentle tools, emotional coaching, and stronger connection during everyday challenges.
