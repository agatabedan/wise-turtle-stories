Privacy Policy

01 Who we are

Wise Turtle Stories is operated by Anna Klimentova. This policy explains how we handle personal information when you use wiseturtle.ca, subscribe to updates, send a Journey request, submit a review, or contact us.

02 Information we collect

We collect information that you choose to provide, including:

  • Newsletter sign-up: your email address.
  • Journey form: your name, email address, selected weather and service, the message you provide about your situation, contact preference, and phone number if you choose phone contact.
  • Book reviews: your name, email address, rating, review text, and your optional consent to be contacted. The name, rating, review text, date, format and any title you provide may be displayed publicly with the review; your email address is not displayed publicly.

We and our hosting providers may also process limited technical information such as IP address, browser/device details, and request information to operate, secure, and prevent abuse of the website. We use browser storage to remember a visitor’s library view and review likes on that visitor’s device. You can clear this in your browser settings.

03 How we use your information

We use personal information only for the purposes connected to the relevant interaction:

  • to send the guide, newsletter, book news, and updates you requested;
  • to respond to Journey requests and provide the contact method you selected;
  • to receive, publish, manage, and respond to reviews, where applicable;
  • to run, maintain, troubleshoot, secure, and improve the website and its forms; and
  • to comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms or protect against fraud or abuse.

We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising. We do not make automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about you.

04 Service providers and external links

We use carefully selected service providers to help operate the site. Depending on the form you use and the services configured at the time, this may include:

  • Render and related infrastructure providers for website hosting and server operations;
  • MailerLite to manage newsletter subscriptions and Journey email workflows;
  • Google Sheets / Google Apps Script to securely organize Journey requests for follow-up;
  • PostgreSQL or another configured database provider to store submitted reviews and Journey requests; and
  • Google Fonts to load the site’s web fonts.

These providers process information only to provide their services to us or as required by law. Some providers may process data outside your province or Canada, where it may be subject to the laws of that jurisdiction.

The website links to third-party sites, including Amazon and social-media platforms. If you follow a link, that service’s own privacy policy applies; Wise Turtle Stories does not control its collection or use of information.

05 Retention and security

We keep personal information only as long as it is needed for the purpose described in this policy, to maintain the relevant record, or to meet legal and operational obligations. For example, newsletter information is retained until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it, and Journey or review records are retained for as long as needed to manage the request, review, or related follow-up.

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information, including access controls and service-provider protections. No internet transmission or storage method is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

06 Your choices and requests

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you, subject to applicable law. You may withdraw consent for newsletter messages at any time using the unsubscribe link in the email or by contacting us. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that occurred before withdrawal.

Canadian commercial emails are sent with consent, sender identification, and an unsubscribe option. Please contact us if you need help with an unsubscribe request or wish to remove a review.

07 Children’s privacy

Our books are made for children, but the website’s sign-up, Journey, and review forms are intended for parents, guardians, educators, and other adults. Please do not submit a child’s personal information through these forms unless you are their parent or guardian and it is necessary for your request.

08 Contact us

For privacy questions, requests, or concerns, contact Anna Klimentova at anna@wiseturtle.ca. You may also contact us through The Story page. We may update this policy when our practices or services change, and will post the revised date above.

This policy is written in plain language to explain our current practices. It does not limit any privacy rights you may have under applicable law.
