Inside the Book

For big feelings that need connection

Warm storytelling for toddlers, parents, and preschool social-emotional learning.

01

The Little Squirrel Who Had Big Feelings

After a full day, Yan falls apart when his emotions flood all at once, and his parents help him calm down through connection.

02

The Little Squirrel Who Felt Lonely

When Yan interrupts, grabs, and demands attention, his parents discover that underneath the behaviour is a simple message: “I need you.”

03

The Little Squirrel When Mama Lost Her Calm

When Mama’s voice grows louder than she meant it to, Yan learns that even grown-ups have big feelings and that love can always be repaired.

Are you navigating toddler tantrums, constant “look at me” moments after a long day, or maybe you had a breakdown yourself and now regret what you said to your little one?

When little ones have big emotions, their nervous system can feel overwhelmed, and so can yours. Crying, yelling, refusing, or falling apart are often signs that a child needs connection.

In Yan the Little Squirrel: Big Feelings, We Calm Together, Little Squirrel Yan and his parents explore emotional regulation, attention-seeking behaviour, and co-regulation through warm storytelling and relatable everyday moments.

Designed for families practicing gentle parenting and preschool social-emotional learning, this book helps children understand big feelings while giving parents practical tools to restore calm and prevent meltdowns.

Perfect for:

  • Toddler books about feelings
  • Books about tantrums and emotional regulation
  • Bedtime calming stories
  • Preschool classroom SEL

Because big feelings don’t need punishment; they need connection.
