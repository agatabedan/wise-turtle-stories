Hi, I’m Anna.

I believe we spend too much time learning how to predict tomorrow’s weather… and too little time learning how to recognize the weather in our own lives.

That’s why I created Wise Turtle: to help children, adults, families, and organizations navigate life’s changing skies with wisdom, courage, and hope.
